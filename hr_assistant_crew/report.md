# Employee Handbook Report
## Summary
This report outlines the key findings related to work arrangements and leave and time off policies within the company. The purpose of this document is to provide a clear and concise overview of the current policies and procedures in place for employees.

## Details
### Work Arrangements
The company operates on a hybrid work model, which requires employees to be in-office for 3 days a week, specifically Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. This arrangement is designed to balance the needs of the business with the flexibility and work-life balance of employees.

### Leave and Time Off
The company provides several types of leave and time off to support employees' well-being and personal needs. The key policies are as follows:
* **Annual Leave**: Employees are entitled to 25 days of annual leave per calendar year. It is essential to note that unused annual leave days do not carry over to the next calendar year.
* **Sick Leave**: The company offers 10 days of paid sick leave. If an employee needs to take more than 3 consecutive days off due to illness, a medical note is required to validate the absence.
* **Annual Leave Accrual for New Employees**: New employees accrue annual leave on a pro-rata basis, with a minimum accrual of 5 days in their first 6 months of service. To become eligible for annual leave, employees must complete 6 months of service.

## Recommendations
Based on the findings, it is recommended that all employees familiarize themselves with these policies to ensure they understand their entitlements and the procedures for requesting leave and time off. Additionally, managers and supervisors should be aware of these policies to provide support and guidance to their team members. The company may also consider reviewing these policies periodically to ensure they remain competitive and aligned with employee needs and business objectives.
